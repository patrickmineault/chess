# Three Mate-in-1 Scenarios for White

```
Back Rank Mate
5k2/4ppp1/8/8/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1
Ra8#
```

```
Smothered Mate
6rk/6pp/7N/8/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

```
Arabian Mate
7k/8/5N2/8/8/8/8/4K2R w - - 0 1
Rh7#
```

**Explanations:**

1. **Back Rank Mate**: The black king on f8 is trapped by its own pawns on e7, f7, and g7. The white rook delivers checkmate on a8, controlling the entire 8th rank.

2. **Smothered Mate**: The black king on h8 is completely surrounded by its own pieces (rook on g8, pawns on g7 and h7). The knight jumps to f7, delivering checkmate with no escape squares available.

3. **Arabian Mate**: The white rook moves to h7, giving check. The knight on f6 controls g8, while the rook controls g7, leaving the black king with no escape from the corner.
