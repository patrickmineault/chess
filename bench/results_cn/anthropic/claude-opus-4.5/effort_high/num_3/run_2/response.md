# Three Mate-in-1 Scenarios for White

Here are three distinct mate-in-1 scenarios:

```
Back Rank Mate
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/8/4R1K1 w - - 0 1
Re8#
```

```
Queen Corner Mate
7k/8/6Q1/8/8/8/8/6K1 w - - 0 1
Qg7#
```

```
Smothered Mate
6rk/6pp/7N/8/8/8/8/K7 w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

**Explanations:**

1. **Back Rank Mate**: The black king on g8 is trapped by its own pawns on f7, g7, and h7. The white rook delivers checkmate by moving to e8, controlling the entire 8th rank.

2. **Queen Corner Mate**: The black king is stuck in the corner on h8. The white queen moves to g7, delivering check while simultaneously covering all escape squares (g8 and h7).

3. **Smothered Mate**: The black king on h8 is "smothered" by its own pieces (rook on g8, pawns on g7 and h7). The white knight jumps to f7, delivering checkmate since the knight attacks h8 and the king has no escape squares.
