# Three Mate-in-1 Scenarios for White

```
Back Rank Mate
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1
Ra8#
```

```
Smothered Mate
6rk/7p/7N/8/8/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

```
Arabian Mate
7k/R7/5N2/8/8/8/8/6K1 w - - 0 1
Rh7#
```

**Explanations:**

1. **Back Rank Mate**: The black king on g8 is trapped by its own pawns on f7, g7, and h7. The white rook delivers checkmate by moving to a8, controlling the entire 8th rank.

2. **Smothered Mate**: The black king on h8 is smothered by its own rook on g8 and pawn on h7. The white knight on h6 jumps to f7, delivering checkmate while also covering the g7 escape square.

3. **Arabian Mate**: The white knight on f6 controls g8 and h7. The white rook moves to h7, delivering checkmate as the black king on h8 cannot escape or capture the defended rook.
