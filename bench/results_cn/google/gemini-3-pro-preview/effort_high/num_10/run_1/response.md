Here are 10 distinct mate-in-1 scenarios for White, ranging from classic patterns to special moves.

### 1. Back Rank Mate
The black King is trapped on the back rank by its own pawns, and the white Rook delivers the fatal blow.

```text
Scenario: Back Rank Mate
FEN: 6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1 w - - 0 1
Re8#
```

### 2. Scholar's Mate Pattern
A classic attack on the weak f7 square using the Queen and Bishop.

```text
Scenario: Scholar's Mate
FEN: r1bqk1nr/pppp1ppp/2n5/2b1p2Q/2B1P3/8/PPPP1PPP/RNB1K1NR w KQkq - 4 4
Qxf7#
```

### 3. Smothered Mate
The black King is completely surrounded by its own pieces, allowing a lone Knight to deliver checkmate.

```text
Scenario: Smothered Mate
FEN: 6rk/6pp/8/6N1/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

### 4. Arabian Mate
A powerful corner mate utilizing the unique coordination between a Rook and a Knight.

```text
Scenario: Arabian Mate
FEN: 7k/R6p/5N2/8/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Rh7#
```

### 5. Anastasia's Mate
The white Knight controls the escape squares (g8, g6), while the Rook delivers the checkmate down the open h-file.

```text
Scenario: Anastasia's Mate
FEN: 5r1k/4N2p/8/8/8/8/8/R6K w - - 0 1
Rh1#
```

### 6. Promotion Mate
The white pawn advances to the final rank, promoting to a Queen (or Rook) to deliver immediate mate.

```text
Scenario: Promotion Mate
FEN: 2r4k/3P4/7K/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1
dxc8=Q#
```

### 7. Kiss of Death
The white Queen approaches right next to the opposing King, protected by her own King.

```text
Scenario: Kiss of Death
FEN: 5k2/5P2/5K2/8/8/8/8/6Q1 w - - 0 1
Qg7#
```

### 8. En Passant Mate
A rare finish where the winning move captures a pawn via the *en passant* rule. Note: The FEN indicates `e6` as the en passant target square.

```text
Scenario: En Passant Mate
FEN: 8/3p4/2pk4/3Pp3/8/8/4K3/8 w - e6 0 1
dxe6#
```

### 9. Two Bishops Mate
The two bishops work in tandem on adjacent diagonals to slice across the board and trap the King in the corner.

```text
Scenario: Two Bishops Mate
FEN: k7/8/K7/8/8/6B1/8/5B2 w - - 0 1
Bg2#
```

### 10. The Ladder Mate
Two major pieces (in this case, Rooks) work together to push the King to the edge of the board; one acts as a wall while the other delivers the checkmate.

```text
Scenario: The Ladder Mate
FEN: k7/4R3/K7/8/3R4/8/8/8 w - - 0 1
Rd8#
```
