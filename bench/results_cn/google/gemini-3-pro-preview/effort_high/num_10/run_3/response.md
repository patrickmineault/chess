Here are 10 distinct mate-in-1 scenarios for White, ranging from basic patterns to more complex tactical motifs.

### 1. Back Rank Mate
In this classic scenario, the black king is trapped behind a wall of its own pawns, and the white rook delivers mate along the back rank.

```text
Scenario 1: Back Rank Mate
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/3R4/6K1 w - - 0 1
Rd8#
```

### 2. Smothered Mate
The black king is completely surrounded ("smothered") by its own pieces, allowing a lone white knight to jump in and deliver checkmate.

```text
Scenario 2: Smothered Mate
6rk/6pp/8/6N1/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

### 3. Arabian Mate
This pattern utilizes a rook and a knight. The knight protects the rook and covers the escape square, while the rook delivers the checkmate in the corner.

```text
Scenario 3: Arabian Mate
7k/R7/5N2/8/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Rh7#
```

### 4. Box Mate (Kiss of Death)
The white queen is placed directly adjacent to the black king, supported by the white king to prevent capture. The box created prevents escape.

```text
Scenario 4: Box Mate
8/5p1p/6pk/8/7P/6P1/5Q2/6K1 w - - 0 1
Qf8#
```

### 5. Boden's Mate
A beautiful mate involving two bishops on criss-crossing diagonals. The king is usually obstructed by its own pieces on the side, allowing the bishops to snipe through the gaps.

```text
Scenario 5: Boden's Mate
2k5/pp1p4/8/2B5/8/8/8/3B2K1 w - - 0 1
Ba6#
```

### 6. Lolli's Mate
In this pattern, a white pawn on f6 (or h6) serves as an anchor, supporting the white queen to deliver mate directly on g7 against a castled king.

```text
Scenario 6: Lolli's Mate
5rk1/5p2/5P2/8/8/8/8/6QK w - - 0 1
Qg7#
```

### 7. Pawn Mate
A rare but satisfying ending where a humble pawn delivers the final blow. Here, the white king cuts off the black king's retreat, and the pawn pushes for the mate.

```text
Scenario 7: Pawn Mate
7k/6pp/5P1K/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1
fxg7#
```

### 8. Promotion Mate
White promotes a pawn to a Queen (or Rook) to instantly deliver checkmate. The existing king on a6 prevents the black king from escaping the corner.

```text
Scenario 8: Promotion Mate
k7/1P6/K7/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1
b8=Q#
```

### 9. Mayet's Mate
This involves a Rook delivering mate while the King is trapped by its own pieces and the file is controlled by the Rook, but the diagonal escape is cut off by a Bishop behind the Rook.

```text
Scenario 9: Mayet's Mate
7k/7p/8/6B1/8/8/8/6RK w - - 0 1
Bf6#
```

### 10. Discovered Double Check Mate
White moves the Knight, which delivers check itself, but also unmasks a check from the Rook on the e-file. Because it is a double check, the King *must* move, but all flight squares are blocked.

```text
Scenario 10: Discovered Double Check Mate
rnb1kbnr/pppp1ppp/8/8/4N3/8/PPPP1PPP/R1B1KB1R w KQkq - 0 1
Nd6#
```
