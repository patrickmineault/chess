Here are 7 distinct mate-in-1 scenarios for white, ranging from classic patterns to specific elemental endgames.

### 1. Back Rank Mate
The "Corridor Mate." The black king is trapped behind its own wall of pawns on the back rank, and the white rook delivers the final blow.

```text
Scenario: Back Rank Mate
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/8/R5K1 w - - 0 1
Ra8#
```

### 2. Smothered Mate
A beautiful pattern where the black king is completely surrounded by its own pieces, and a lone white knight jumps in to deliver checkmate.

```text
Scenario: Smothered Mate
6rk/6pp/8/4N3/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

### 3. Scholar's Mate (Battery Mate)
A classic opening disaster. The white queen, supported by the bishop (forming a battery), captures the weak f7 pawn.

```text
Scenario: Scholar's Mate
r1bqk1nr/pppp1ppp/2n5/2b1p2Q/2B1P3/8/PPPP1PPP/RNB1K1NR w KQkq - 0 1
Qxf7#
```

### 4. Anastasia's Mate
A specific mating net on the side of the board. The white knight controls escape squares g8 and g6, while the rook delivers the fatal check down the h-file.

```text
Scenario: Anastasia's Mate
5r2/4N1pk/8/R7/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Rh5#
```

### 5. Arabian Mate
A powerful endgame pattern featuring a rook and knight. The knight protects the rook and cuts off the king's escape, engaging the cornered king.

```text
Scenario: Arabian Mate
7k/6p1/5N2/8/8/8/R7/5K2 w - - 0 1
Rh2#
```

### 6. Lawnmower Mate (Ladder Mate)
Two major pieces (here, rooks) work together to push the king to the edge. One rook acts as a fence on the 7th rank, while the other delivers the check on the 8th.

```text
Scenario: Lawnmower Mate
4k3/R7/8/8/8/8/8/1R2K3 w - - 0 1
Rb8#
```

### 7. Pawn Mate
A rare but satisfying finish. The white king provides opposition, preventing the black king from moving forward or sideways, while the pawn pushes for the kill.

```text
Scenario: Pawn Mate
8/8/7p/5K1k/7p/6P1/8/8 w - - 0 1
g4#
```
