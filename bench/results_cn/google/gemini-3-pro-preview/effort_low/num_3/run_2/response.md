Here are three mate-in-1 scenarios for white involving different tactical motifs.

### Scenario 1: Back Rank Mate
In this classic scenario, the black king is trapped behind a wall of its own pawns, allowing the white rook to deliver checkmate on the final rank.

```text
The Back Rank Mate
6k1/5ppp/8/8/8/8/5PPP/4R1K1 w - - 0 1
Re8#
```

### Scenario 2: Smothered Mate
This scenario features a knight delivering checkmate to a king that is completely surrounded ("smothered") by its own pieces, preventing escape.

```text
The Smothered Mate
6rk/6pp/8/6N1/8/8/8/7K w - - 0 1
Nf7#
```

### Scenario 3: Scholar's Mate Finish
This scenario replicates the final position of the "Scholar's Mate" (or a similar attack on f7), where the Queen, supported by a Bishop (or another piece), delivers mate on the weak f7 square.

```text
Attack on f7
r1bqk1nr/pppp1Qpp/2n5/2b1p3/2B1P3/8/PPPP1PPP/RNB1K1NR w KQkq - 0 1
Qxf7#
```
